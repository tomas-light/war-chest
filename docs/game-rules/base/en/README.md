## Objective and contents

In a two-player game, win by controlling 6 locations simultaneously.
In a four-player game, a team wins by controlling 8 locations simultaneously.
The game ends immediately when the final required location is controlled,
without waiting for the turn or round to end. Starting locations count.
Destroying an army or exhausting a bag is not itself a victory condition.

## Terms

Recruit = наём; deploy = розыгрыш; bolster = усиление; control = захват;
claim initiative = перехват инициативы; pass = пас. Move, attack, control
and tactics are maneuvers. A turn consists of one main coin action and all
of its resulting effects. A round consists of turns until all hands are
exhausted. Abilities can provide additional coins or actions within a turn.

A unit on the board includes its bolstering coins. Normally a player may
have only one unit of each type on the board, unless its card says otherwise.
Supply, bag, hand, discard pile, board and removed coins are distinct zones.

## Information and rule precedence

Hands are hidden from other players. A face-down discard does not reveal the
type spent. Face-up discards remain visible until returned to the bag.
You may inspect your own discard pile, including face-down coins (explicit
clarification in the Russian rulebook, p. 11). A recruited coin is always
revealed and placed face-up in the discard pile.

A Royal Coin is not an ordinary unit coin. It can pay for face-down actions,
or be used for the Royal Guard's special tactic when permitted. It is not
the Initiative Marker. Your Royal Coin circulates through your bag, hand and
discard pile; the single shared Initiative Marker identifies the initiative
holder and never enters a bag. Any hand coin can pay to claim initiative,
not just a Royal Coin.

Unit cards provide exceptions to the general rules. Unsupported abilities
in the web application must not silently become simplified tabletop rules.
