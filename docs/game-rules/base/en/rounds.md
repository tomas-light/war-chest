# Rounds and turns

[Base game](./README.md) · [Русский](../ru/rounds.md) · Sources: PDFs, pp. 6–7, 9.

## Drawing coins

At the start of a round, each player randomly draws 3 coins without showing
their hand. Whenever you need another coin and your bag is empty, return your
entire discard pile to the bag, randomize it, and continue drawing up to three.
Do not replenish early while coins remain in the bag. If the bag and discard
pile together contain fewer than needed, draw all available coins, possibly zero.

The same replenishment procedure applies when an ability requires a draw from
an empty bag. Coins on the board, in supply or removed from the game are not
returned to the bag this way.

## Taking turns

The initiative holder at the start of the round acts first. Duel players
alternate; four players follow a fixed circular order alternating teams.
Claiming initiative during a round changes who starts the next round only.

On your turn, choose one coin and a legal main action. Fully resolve any
resulting actions, including mandatory steps and decisions about optional
steps, before play passes on. An ability can allow multiple coins to be used
within that same turn.

A player with no coins in hand skips their opportunity without payment; this
does not eliminate them. The round ends when all hands are empty and the
current chain of effects is complete. Then draw for the next round. If a win
condition is met earlier, the game ends immediately.

## Face-down actions

Discard any hand coin face-down, including a Royal Coin, to perform one action:

- **Pass.** Do nothing else. You may still use remaining hand coins on later turns.
- **Claim initiative.** Only if an opponent currently holds it and you have not
  already held it during this round. Thus it can change hands only once per
  round; the side that began with it cannot reclaim it. In team play you may
  not claim it from your teammate.
- **Recruit.** After payment, choose a coin of one of your unit types from your
  own supply, reveal it, and place it face-up in your discard pile. Payment
  need not match the recruited type. You cannot recruit a coin absent from
  supply. The new coin does not go directly into your hand or onto the board.
