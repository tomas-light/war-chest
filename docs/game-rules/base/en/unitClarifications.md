# Unit clarifications

[Base game](./README.md) · [Русский](../ru/unitClarifications.md).
Sources: both PDFs, pp. 16–18. This FAQ does not replace full unit cards.

## Range and movement

- **Archer:** attacks only using its tactic, targeting a unit exactly two spaces
  away; an ordinary adjacent attack is unavailable.
- **Crossbowman:** may attack an adjacent target normally or use its tactic
  two spaces along a straight line. The intervening space must be empty.
- **Ensign:** grants an ordinary one-space move ending within two spaces of
  Ensign, not teleportation. Berserker's attribute may trigger. Do not confuse
  the base Ensign with Nobility's Bannerman.
- **Lancer:** a legal attack target must exist when choosing the tactic. Its
  one- or two-space move and subsequent attack follow the same straight line.
  It cannot use the tactic merely to move. An unbolstered Lancer cannot choose
  Knight as that attack's target. Lancer has no ordinary attack.
- **Light Cavalry:** may move one space normally or two using its tactic.
  The usual requirement for empty intermediate spaces still applies.

## Additional maneuvers

- **Berserker:** after paying one hand coin, further maneuvers are paid for by
  removing coins from that unit itself, not additional hand coins. Resolve
  those maneuvers consecutively.
- **Footman:** its two units may perform different maneuvers during the tactic,
  such as one controlling while the other moves.
- **Marshal:** grants an ordinary attack, not another unit's tactic. It cannot
  grant Archer or Lancer their prohibited ordinary attack. Warrior Priest,
  Berserker and Swordsman attributes may still trigger.
- **Mercenary:** the free maneuver following recruitment requires its unit
  already to be on the board. A free maneuver is not deployment or recruitment.

## Losses and draws

- **Pikeman:** the retaliatory coin loss in an adjacent attack is simultaneous
  with Pikeman's loss, even if Pikeman is destroyed. It is not an attack, so
  Knight's protection against unbolstered attackers does not prevent it.
  The attacker's last coin may also be removed.
- **Warrior Priest:** the coin drawn after attacking/controlling must be used
  immediately, including the option to pass; it cannot be saved for a later
  turn. The Russian edition explicitly confirms that the draw still occurs
  if Pikeman's retaliation destroys Warrior Priest.
- Enemy starting locations may be controlled; their origin gives no immunity.

Other full card abilities and edge-case combinations require individual card
review; do not infer missing rules from text absent from the PDFs.
